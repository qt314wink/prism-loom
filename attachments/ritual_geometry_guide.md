## Guide Overview

This guide is part of a multi-module laser-cut mandala curriculum and product system centered on Ritual Geometry, with bonus assets designed for printable use, creative iteration, and commercial packaging.[cite:605] It includes four coordinated asset classes: coloring page prompts, stencil prompts, wayfinder and pencil-guide prompts, and a 50-card remix prompt set.[cite:605]

The project sits at the intersection of generative art, SVG and GCode-oriented fabrication workflows, and productized creative tooling for digital distribution.[cite:605][cite:610][cite:611] The prompts are organized to support both aesthetic consistency and practical reuse across drawing, tracing, laser preparation, and educational formats.[cite:605]

## Visual Language

Use one consistent visual language across all assets: sacred geometry, radial symmetry, crisp black linework, clean white background, centered composition, no mockup scene, no shadows, no gradients, no photorealism, and enough negative space for printing or tracing. For coloring pages, the lines should feel inviting and meditative; for stencils, they should feel bold and cuttable; for wayfinders, they should feel instructional and lightly structured.

A simple base suffix you can append to almost everything is: **“clean vector line art, black ink on white background, centered composition, radial symmetry, printable page layout, no shading, no gray tones, no texture, no mockup, high clarity, SVG-friendly”**. When you want a more premium editorial feel, add **“museum-grade geometric composition, calm negative space, precise line hierarchy.”**

These are instructional underlays rather than finished artwork. They support alignment, scaling, radial construction, repetition, and tracing workflows relevant to hand drawing and fabrication prep.[cite:610][cite:611]

1. **12-Segment Radial Guide** — Minimal circular pencil guide with 12 equal radial divisions, concentric rings, alignment marks, and central anchor point.
2. **8-Petal Construction Guide** — Geometric flower guide with petal axes, inner and outer circles, and light registration points for hand drawing.
3. **Mandala Layer Registration Sheet** — Precision wayfinder with concentric placement rings, cardinal direction markers, and layer alignment ticks.
4. **Outer Ring Layout Guide** — Printable circular guide for building clean perimeter bands, evenly spaced segment marks, and subtle pencil-friendly line weights.
5. **Centerfinding Rosette Guide** — Central rosette construction page with crosshairs, ring spacing, and petal placement arcs.
6. **Compass Mandala Draft Grid** — Radial grid with 6, 12, and 24 division options, intended for sketching and tracing over.
7. **Stencil Placement Guide** — Instructional page showing where a stencil can rotate and repeat, including faint reference circles and position marks.
8. **Symmetry Training Sheet** — Half-detailed radial guide encouraging users to complete the remaining pattern by following mirrored axes.
9. **Petal Expansion Guide** — Pencil scaffold that shows how to scale petals outward ring by ring with consistent spacing.
10. **Vesica Construction Wheel** — Mandala drafting guide based on overlapping circles, faint enough for pencil but clear enough for education.
11. **Layer Stack Guide** — Printable page with 3-layer, 5-layer, and 7-layer spacing references for planning physical mandala builds.
12. **Edge Accent Guide** — Sheet for testing outer ring embellishments, border rhythms, and repeat motifs before final drawing.

For these, add a suffix like: **“instructional drafting page, light line hierarchy, geometric construction marks, pencil-friendly, printable, clean white background, no decorative shading.”**

## Prompt Families

A stronger bundle comes from building assets in families so one motif becomes a coloring page, a stencil, and a guide. That makes the system feel authored rather than randomly assembled.

For example: Quiet Bloom becomes a full coloring page, Quiet Bloom Stencil becomes the bold simplified version, and Quiet Bloom Guide becomes the 12-segment construction sheet. The same family logic can be applied to Burning Lattice, Deep Well, Golden Coil, Radiant Gate, and Vesica Field.

## Production Notes

The strongest version of this project is a language system rather than a loose pack of assets. Each page, guide, stencil, and remix card should feel like it belongs to the same world through naming, geometry, line logic, and family structure.[cite:605]

The prompt set also aligns with a project direction that includes SVG and GCode outputs for mandala and laser-cutting workflows, making the guide relevant both as a printable bonus asset and as a design-development tool for fabrication-oriented work.[cite:610][cite:611]
